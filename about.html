About Apex Triad

An apparel brand built for people who train with intent, not just for show.

Apex Triad makes training apparel and gear for people who take their effort seriously — not gym-influencer content, just gear that holds up to real work.

We're a new, independent brand, run directly by the people who design it. Every order right now is fulfilled personally, which means slower shipping than a big retailer, but also means you're supporting something built from scratch rather than a warehouse.

Built to be used

Gear is tested for actual training, not just photos. If it can't take a real session, it doesn't go in the store.

Small batch, honest

We'd rather sell out of a small run than overpromise on stock we can't deliver on time.

Direct to you

No middlemen right now — orders, questions, and feedback all come straight to us.
